<p><strong><em>Magic Resistance.</em></strong> The arcanaloth has <span class="highlight">advantage</span> on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.</p>
<p><strong><em>Magic Weapons.</em></strong> The arcanaloth’s weapon attacks are magical.</p>
<p><strong><em>Mind Blank.</em></strong> The arcanaloth is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions, read its thoughts, reveal its location through divination magic, or cause it to become <span class="highlight">charmed</span>.</p>
<p><strong><em>Archive Magic.</em></strong> Outside of combat, the arcanaloth can cast <em>alter self</em>, <em>contact other plane</em>, <em>darkness</em>, <em>detect magic</em>, <em>detect thoughts</em>, <em>identify</em>, <em>invisibility</em> (self only), <em>minor illusion</em>, <em>prestidigitation</em>, <em>suggestion</em>, and <em>Tenser’s floating disk</em>, requiring no material components. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence.</p>
<h3>Actions</h3>
<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The arcanaloth makes two attacks, only one of which can be a <strong><em>claws</em></strong> attack. It can replace one attack with <strong><em>phantasmal writ</em></strong> or <strong><em>edict of terror</em></strong>.</p>
<p><strong><em>Claws.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. <strong>Hit:</strong> <span class="highlight">8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage</span> plus <span class="highlight">10 (3d6) poison damage</span>, and the target must succeed on a <span class="highlight">DC 17 Constitution saving throw</span> or be <span class="highlight">poisoned</span> until the end of its next turn.</p>
<p><strong><em>Hellfire Bolt.</em></strong> <em>Ranged Spell Attack:</em> +9 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. <strong>Hit:</strong> <span class="highlight">16 (2d10 + 5) fire damage</span>. If the target is concentrating on a spell, it has <span class="highlight">disadvantage</span> on the saving throw it makes to maintain concentration.</p>
<p><strong><em>Unerring Missiles.</em></strong> The arcanaloth conjures three darts of arcane force. Each dart strikes a creature the arcanaloth can see within <span class="highlight">120 feet</span> and deals <span class="highlight">6 (1d4 + 4) force damage</span>. The darts strike simultaneously, and the arcanaloth can direct them to hit one creature or several.</p>
<p><strong><em>Phantasmal Writ.</em></strong> One creature the arcanaloth can see within <span class="highlight">60 feet</span> must make a <span class="highlight">DC 17 Intelligence saving throw</span>. On a failed save, the target takes <span class="highlight">14 (4d6) psychic damage</span>, and the arcanaloth chooses one object the target is wearing or carrying. Until the end of the target’s next turn, the target believes that object is cursed, venomous, or otherwise dangerous and can’t willingly use it. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage and suffers no additional effect.</p>
<p><strong><em>Edict of Terror.</em></strong> Each creature of the arcanaloth’s choice within <span class="highlight">30 feet</span> that can hear it must succeed on a <span class="highlight">DC 17 Wisdom saving throw</span> or take <span class="highlight">10 (3d6) psychic damage</span> and become <span class="highlight">frightened</span> of the arcanaloth until the end of the arcanaloth’s next turn. A creature that fails the saving throw by <span class="highlight">5 or more</span> must also use its reaction, if available, to move up to half its speed directly away from the arcanaloth by the safest available route.</p>
<p><strong><em>Chain Lightning (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> Lightning erupts from the arcanaloth toward one creature it can see within <span class="highlight">150 feet</span>, then arcs to up to three other creatures of the arcanaloth’s choice within <span class="highlight">30 feet</span> of the first target. Each target must make a <span class="highlight">DC 17 Dexterity saving throw</span>, taking <span class="highlight">35 (10d6) lightning damage</span> on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.</p>
<h3>Bonus Actions</h3>
<p><strong><em>Teleport.</em></strong> The arcanaloth magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to <span class="highlight">60 feet</span> to an unoccupied space it can see.</p>
<p><strong><em>Mirror Veil.</em></strong> The arcanaloth surrounds itself with illusory duplicates until the start of its next turn. The first attack roll made against it before then has <span class="highlight">disadvantage</span>. If that attack misses, the arcanaloth can immediately move up to <span class="highlight">15 feet</span> without provoking opportunity attacks.</p>
<p><strong><em>Contractual Command.</em></strong> One creature the arcanaloth can see within <span class="highlight">60 feet</span> that can hear it must succeed on a <span class="highlight">DC 17 Wisdom saving throw</span> or obey one of the following commands until the start of the arcanaloth’s next turn: <em>Approach</em>, <em>Drop</em>, <em>Flee</em>, or <em>Grovel</em>. A creature has <span class="highlight">advantage</span> on this saving throw if the arcanaloth or one of its allies has damaged it since the end of the arcanaloth’s last turn.</p>
<h3>Reactions</h3>
<p><strong><em>Arcane Interdiction.</em></strong> <strong>Trigger:</strong> The arcanaloth is hit by an attack, or it sees a creature within <span class="highlight">60 feet</span> cast a spell. <strong>Effect:</strong> If the triggering event is an attack, the arcanaloth gains a <span class="highlight">+5 bonus to AC</span> against that attack, potentially causing it to miss. If the triggering event is a spell of 3rd level or lower, the spell fails and has no effect. If the spell is 4th level or higher, the arcanaloth makes an Intelligence check with a DC equal to <span class="highlight">10 + the spell’s level</span>, causing the spell to fail and have no effect on a success.</p>
