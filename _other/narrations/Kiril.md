### Second Phase: The Heart Devoured

At death’s threshold, Kiril kneels.

With a roar that drowns the storm, he buries his claws into his own ribcage. Bone cracks. Blood fountains black upon the stones. He tears free his still-beating heart and raises it to the moon like a blasphemous sacrament.

Then he devours it.

His spine snaps backward as he doubles in height, sinew stretching, flesh warping. Bony spurs erupt from his shoulders like a mockery of angelic wings. His skull swells grotesquely; his jaws split and multiply, teeth blossoming where eyes once wept hatred. The ground trembles beneath his newfound bulk.

The beast reborn stands fresh in its horror, and the night recoils.
